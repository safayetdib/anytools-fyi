# Spec: launch SEO, AI search and metadata

Status: approved by the owner on 2026-11-06. Launch with only Merge PDF working.

## Indexing
- Built tool pages, the home page, All tools, the PDF hub and the info pages are indexed.
- Unbuilt tool pages stay reachable as "Coming soon" but carry `noindex`, have no app schema, and are left out of the sitemap and llms.txt.
- A hub is indexed only when it has at least one built tool.
- 404 is `noindex`.
- Unbuilt tools show a "Coming soon" label on their cards. Header quick links show built tools only.

## Files for crawlers
- `/sitemap.xml`: every indexable page, with `lastmod` from its `updated` date.
- `/robots.txt`: allow all crawlers, AI crawlers included, and point to the sitemap.
- `/llms.txt`: a plain summary of the site and its indexable pages for AI search, per llmstxt.org.
- `/llms-full.txt`: the same, plus each built tool's full guide (steps, limits, tips, FAQ).

## Page metadata
- Unique title (at most 60 characters) and description (70 to 160 characters) on every indexable page, checked by a unit test.
- Canonical URL, Open Graph (type, site name, title, description, URL, locale, image with size and alt) and Twitter `summary_large_image`.
- A 1200x630 share image for the site and for each built tool, generated by `scripts/build-og.mjs`.
- `theme-color` for light and dark, `application-name`, `apple-mobile-web-app-title`.

## Icons
- `favicon.ico` (32px) for older browsers, SVG favicon, Apple touch icon, 192 and 512 app icons, and a 512 maskable icon in the manifest.

## Structured data
- Home: WebSite (with publisher) and Organization (PNG logo).
- Built tool: WebApplication (with image and feature list), HowTo from the steps, FAQPage, BreadcrumbList.
- Hubs and All tools: CollectionPage and BreadcrumbList.
