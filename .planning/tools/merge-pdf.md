# Tool brief: Merge PDF

- **Target searches:** "merge pdf", "combine pdf", "join pdf files", "pdf merger", Bangla "পিডিএফ যুক্ত করুন".
- **Who and why:** students, job seekers and office workers combining scanned pages, certificates or forms into one file for an upload or email.
- **What competitors lack:** the big merge sites upload files to their servers, many add limits or ask for signup on larger jobs, and most say little about where files go.
- **Our gap:** files never leave the device, no limits beyond the device's memory, and plain answers about privacy.
- **Library:** pdf-lib 1.17.1 (MIT), run in a Web Worker and loaded only when the visitor starts choosing files.
- **Limits:** PDF files only, 100 MB per file, warning above 30 MB, at least 2 files. Password-protected PDFs are refused with a clear message.
- **Known loss:** bookmarks and form fields from the original files may not carry over. The page says so.
- **Privacy note:** files are read and merged in the browser and saved straight to the device.
- **FAQ:** Does this upload my files? Does it work offline? Is there a limit on files or size? Can I merge password-protected PDFs? Will my bookmarks and links be kept?
