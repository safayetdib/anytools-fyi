faq:
  - question: "Does this upload my PDF?"
    answer: "No. Your PDF is read and numbered inside your browser, and the new file is saved straight to your device. You can check this in your browser's Network tab: no file is uploaded."
  - question: "How do I start numbering from page 2?"
    answer: "Set Number from page to 2. The first page is left without a number, and page 2 gets the First number, usually 1."
  - question: "What do {n} and {p} mean in custom text?"
    answer: "{n} is the page's number and {p} is the last number printed. For example, Page {n} of {p} gives Page 3 of 10."
  - question: "What are facing pages?"
    answer: "Pages printed as a book, where pages sit side by side in spreads. Numbers then go on the outer edge of each page, left on left pages and right on right pages."
  - question: "Can I write the numbers in Bangla?"

  - question: "Will the rest of my PDF change?"
    answer: "No. Only the numbers are added on top. Text, images, links and page sizes stay exactly as they were."
---

Adding page numbers makes a long PDF easy to read, print and talk about, like a report, a thesis or an application with many pages. Put the numbers where you want them, skip the cover, write Page 1 of 10 or your own words, and pick the font, size and colour. The numbers are added as real text, so they stay sharp and searchable, and the rest of your PDF isn't changed.
