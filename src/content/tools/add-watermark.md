faq:
  - question: "Does this upload my PDF?"
    answer: "No. Your PDF is read and stamped inside your browser, and the new file is saved straight to your device. You can check this in your browser's Network tab: no file is uploaded."
  - question: "Can I use my logo as a watermark?"
    answer: "Yes. Choose Place image and pick a JPG, PNG, WebP or GIF. PNG logos keep their see-through background, so only the logo itself shows on the page."
  - question: "What does Transparency do?"
    answer: "It makes the watermark see-through. At 75%, it's drawn at a quarter of its strength, so the page underneath stays easy to read."
  - question: "Can a watermark be removed later?"
    answer: "It's added into the page itself, so it can't be switched off like a comment. Mosaic, which covers the whole page, is the hardest to edit out."
  - question: "Can I write the watermark in Bangla?"
    answer: "Yes. Type it in the Text box, such as গোপনীয়. Bangla is drawn as a sharp picture so its letters join correctly in every PDF reader; it can't be selected or searched like Latin text."
  - question: "Will the rest of my PDF change?"
    answer: "No. Only the watermark is added. Text, images, links and page sizes stay exactly as they were."
---

Adding a watermark marks a PDF as yours or shows its status: CONFIDENTIAL on a contract, DRAFT on a report, or your logo on every page. Choose text or an image, put it in one spot or repeat it across the page, and set how see-through and how turned it is. You see the result on your pages before you save, and the rest of your PDF isn't changed.
