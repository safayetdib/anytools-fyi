faq:
  - question: "Does this upload my PDF?"
    answer: "No. The text is read and converted inside your browser and saved straight to your device. You can check this in your browser's Network tab: nothing is uploaded."
  - question: "Why does Bangla copy out of my PDF as Latin letters?"
    answer: "The PDF was typed with Bijoy, in a font like SutonnyMJ. Bijoy fonts draw Bangla letters in place of Latin ones, so the PDF stores \"Avgvi\" and only shows আমার. Copying gives you what's stored."
  - question: "Which fonts does it convert?"
    answer: "SutonnyMJ, SutonnyOMJ, TonnyMJ and the other fonts whose names end in MJ, and Bangla fonts marked ANSI. English text and Bangla already in Unicode, such as Kalpurush or Nikosh, are left as they are."
  - question: "How accurate is it?"
    answer: "Letters, joined letters, vowel signs and র্ come out right in nearly every word. A few rare joined letters look the same as two separate letters in Bijoy, so check names and unusual words."
  - question: "Can I edit the result in Word?"
    answer: "Yes. Choose Word, and the Bangla is set in Nirmala UI, which comes with Windows. Any Unicode Bangla font, like Kalpurush or Nikosh, works too."
  - question: "Does it work offline?"
    answer: "Yes, once your files are chosen. The page loads its PDF tools as soon as you start choosing files. After that, you can turn off your connection and still convert them."
---

Converting a Bijoy PDF to Unicode fixes Bangla that copies out as Latin letters, like Avgvi instead of আমার. Text typed in SutonnyMJ and the other Bijoy fonts is turned into Unicode Bangla you can copy, search, paste anywhere and edit, saved as text or as a Word document. English and Unicode Bangla stay as they are, and nothing ever leaves your device.
