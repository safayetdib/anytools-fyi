faq:
  - question: "Does this upload my PDFs?"
    answer: "No. Both PDFs are compared inside your browser, and the result is saved straight to your device. You can check this in your browser's Network tab: nothing is uploaded."
  - question: "What does it compare?"
    answer: "The words, in reading order. A word changed, added or taken out is highlighted. Changes to pictures, colours or layout aren't shown."
  - question: "Can it compare scanned documents?"
    answer: "Scans are pictures with no words to read. Run each through OCR PDF first, then compare the searchable copies."
  - question: "Why is a whole paragraph highlighted?"
    answer: "When a paragraph moves, or most of its words change, it shows as removed where it was and added where it is now."
  - question: "Do both PDFs need the same number of pages?"
    answer: "No. Pages are shown in pairs; a page only one version has sits beside an empty space, and words are compared across pages."
  - question: "Can I compare more than two?"
    answer: "Two at a time. To check a third version, compare it with the second as a new pair."

Comparing PDFs shows what changed between two versions of a document: every word taken out, put in or changed, highlighted on the pages side by side. It's quick to check a contract, a policy or a report before you sign or share it, in English or Bangla. Nothing ever leaves your device.
