faq:
  - question: "Does this upload my PDF?"
    answer: "No. Your PDF is cropped inside your browser and saved straight to your device. You can check this in your browser's Network tab: nothing is uploaded."
  - question: "Is the cut-away part deleted?"
    answer: "The pages are resized to the box, so the cut-away part no longer shows or prints in any PDF reader. Like other PDF croppers, the hidden part stays inside the file, so don't rely on cropping to hide private details."
  - question: "Can I crop just one page?"
    answer: "Yes. Choose Current page, move to the page with the arrows, and drag its box. Pages you don't change stay as they are."
  - question: "Will my text stay sharp?"
    answer: "Yes. Cropping only changes the page size. Text, pictures and links stay exactly as they were, so you can still select and search the text."
  - question: "What does Fit to content do?"
    answer: "It looks at the page as shown and moves the box to just around the text and pictures, leaving a little room. It's the quickest way to cut away white margins."
  - question: "Does it work offline?"
    answer: "Yes, once your file is chosen. The page loads its PDF tool as soon as you start choosing a file. After that, you can turn off your connection and still crop it."
---

Cropping a PDF cuts away what you don't need around the edges: wide white margins on scans, crop marks on print files, or everything except one chart or picture. Drag a box over the part to keep, or let Fit to content find it, and crop every page the same way or each page on its own. Text stays sharp and selectable, and the file never leaves your device.
