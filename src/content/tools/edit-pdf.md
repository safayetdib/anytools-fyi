faq:
  - question: "Does this upload my PDF?"

  - question: "Can I change the text that's already in the PDF?"

Editing a PDF lets you add text, pictures, shapes, highlights and drawings to its pages, or cover parts with white, without any other app. Type in English or Bangla, place a logo or stamp, mark up a contract, or fix a typo by covering it and typing over it. Everything you add is drawn into the PDF exactly where you put it, and nothing ever leaves your device.
