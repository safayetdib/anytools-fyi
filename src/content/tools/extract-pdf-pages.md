---

Extracting pages saves pages from a PDF as new files: every page as its own PDF, or only the pages you choose, kept apart or merged into one. It's useful for sending one certificate from a bundle, sharing a single chapter, or keeping the pages a form asks for. Every page stays at its original quality, and your original file isn't changed.
