faq:
  - question: "Does this upload my form?"
    answer: "No. Your form is filled in inside your browser and saved straight to your device. You can check this in your browser's Network tab: nothing is uploaded."

Filling in a PDF form lets you type into its own boxes, tick its choices and pick from its lists, then save it ready to send, without printing or another app. Answers can be in English or Bangla, kept editable or locked into the page. Nothing ever leaves your device.
