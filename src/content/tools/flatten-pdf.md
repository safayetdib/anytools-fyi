faq:
  - question: "Does this upload my PDF?"
    answer: "No. The PDF is flattened inside your browser and saved straight to your device. You can check this in your browser's Network tab: nothing is uploaded."
  - question: "What does flattening a PDF do?"
    answer: "It draws form answers and comments into the page itself, like ink on paper, then removes the form fields and comments. The PDF looks the same, but nothing can be typed in, changed or deleted."
  - question: "Will my answers still show?"

  - question: "Does it work offline?"

---

Flattening a PDF makes its filled-in form and its comments part of the page, so they can't be changed, moved or deleted. Answers, ticks, highlights, notes and stamps look just as before, and links keep working. Flatten one PDF or many at once, and nothing ever leaves your device.
