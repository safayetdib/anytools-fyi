faq:
  - question: "Does this upload my photos?"
    answer: "No. Your HEIC photos are read and turned into a PDF inside your browser, and the PDF is saved straight to your device. Nothing is uploaded."
  - question: "What is a HEIC file?"
    answer: "HEIC (or HEIF) is the format iPhones and iPads use for photos, because it keeps the same quality in about half the space of JPG. Windows, Android and many apps can't open it."
  - question: "Does it work on Windows and Android?"
    answer: "Yes. Chrome, Edge and Firefox can't open HEIC by themselves, so the page loads a small HEIC reader (about 0.8 MB) the first time you add one. It runs on your device, like the rest of the tool."
  - question: "Will my photos lose quality?"
    answer: "Very little. Each HEIC photo is converted once at high quality before it goes into the PDF."
  - question: "Can I mix HEIC with JPG and PNG?"
    answer: "Yes. Add them together and they become pages of one PDF, in the order of the list."
  - question: "Can I stop my iPhone saving HEIC?"
    answer: "Yes. In Settings, then Camera, then Formats, choose Most Compatible. New photos are then saved as JPG."
---

Converting HEIC to PDF turns iPhone and iPad photos into a document that opens on any device, including Windows and Android, which can't open HEIC themselves. Add your photos, set their order, choose A4, US Letter or each photo's own size, and download one PDF. Photos come out upright and at high quality, and they never leave your device.
