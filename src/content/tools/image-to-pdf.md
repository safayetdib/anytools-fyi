faq:
  - question: "Does this upload my images?"
    answer: "No. Your images are read and turned into a PDF inside your browser, and the PDF is saved straight to your device. Nothing is uploaded."
  - question: "How do I make one PDF from photos of a document?"

  - question: "Which image types can I use?"

---

Converting images to PDF turns photos, screenshots and scans into one document that's easy to send, upload or print, which is what most forms and portals ask for. Add JPG, PNG, WebP and other images, set their order, choose A4, US Letter or each image's own size, and download a single PDF. Photos keep their quality and come out upright, just as your phone shows them.
