Merging joins the pages of several PDFs into one file, in the order you choose. It's useful when a form or email asks for a single file: certificates for a job application, scanned pages of one document, or receipts for an expense claim. After merging, you can check every page, turn sideways scans upright, remove pages you don't need and make every page the same size, all before you download.
