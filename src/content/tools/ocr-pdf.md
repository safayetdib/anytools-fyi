faq:
  - question: "Does this upload my PDF?"
    answer: "No. Every page is read inside your browser and the result is saved straight to your device. The reader itself downloads from this site the first time; your files never leave your device."
  - question: "Can it read Bangla?"
    answer: "Yes. Tick Bangla to read printed Bangla text, and tick English too for documents that mix both. Joined letters and vowel signs come out as normal Bangla text you can copy."
  - question: "Will my PDF look different?"
    answer: "No. The text is added invisibly over each page, where each word was read, so the pages, pictures and quality stay exactly as they were."
  - question: "Why is the first time slower?"
    answer: "The reader for each language downloads once (English 3 MB, Bangla 1.4 MB). Your browser keeps it, so later files start right away, even offline."
  - question: "Can it read handwriting?"
    answer: "Rarely well. OCR is made for printed and typed text. Neat block letters sometimes work; joined handwriting usually doesn't."
  - question: "Does the file get bigger?"
    answer: "Only a little. The text adds a few kilobytes for each page, far less than the pictures of the pages themselves."
---

OCR (optical character recognition) turns scanned PDFs, which are only pictures of pages, into documents you can search, select and copy text from. It reads printed English and Bangla, adds the words invisibly where they appear, and leaves every page looking exactly as it did. Read one PDF or many at once, and nothing ever leaves your device.
