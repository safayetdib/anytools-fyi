faq:
  - question: "Does this upload my files?"
    answer: "No. Your PDFs are read and rearranged inside your browser, and the new file is saved straight to your device. You can check this in your browser's Network tab: no file is uploaded."
  - question: "Can I combine pages from different PDFs?"
    answer: "Yes. Add several files and their pages all appear in one grid, each marked with its file's letter. Drag them into any order to make one new PDF."
  - question: "How do I move pages on a phone?"
    answer: "Press and drag the dots under a page. The page lands where the green line shows when you let go."
  - question: "Will the pages lose quality?"
    answer: "No. Pages are copied exactly as they are, with the same text, images and size. Turning a page only changes the direction it's shown in."
  - question: "Does it work offline?"
    answer: "Yes, once your files are chosen. The page loads its PDF tool as soon as you start choosing files. After that, you can turn off your connection and still organize them."
  - question: "Can I organize password-protected PDFs?"
    answer: "Not yet. They're marked Has a password as soon as you add them. Remove the password in the app that created the file, then try again here."
---

Organizing puts the pages of a PDF in the order you need: move pages, turn the sideways ones, delete what you don't need, and add blank pages. You can also add several PDFs and mix their pages into one new file. Every page keeps its original quality, and your original files aren't changed.
