  - question: "Does this upload my PDF?"
    answer: "No. Your PDF is read and drawn as pictures inside your browser, and the pictures are saved straight to your device. You can check this in your browser's Network tab: nothing is uploaded."

Converting PDF to JPG turns pages into pictures you can share in chats, post online or put in a presentation, where a PDF can't go. Save every page as a JPG, or extract just the photos placed inside the PDF. Choose Normal for screens or High for print, and download one picture or all of them in one ZIP.
