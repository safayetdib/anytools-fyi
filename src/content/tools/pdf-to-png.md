faq:
  - question: "Does this upload my PDF?"
    answer: "No. Your PDF is read and drawn as pictures inside your browser, and the pictures are saved straight to your device. You can check this in your browser's Network tab: nothing is uploaded."
  - question: "Can I get a transparent background?"
    answer: "Yes. Under Page background, choose Transparent. Wherever the page has no text or pictures, the PNG is see-through. Scanned pages are photos of paper, so they stay white."
  - question: "Why are PNG files bigger than JPG?"
    answer: "PNG is lossless: it keeps every pixel exactly. That makes text razor sharp, but photos take more space. Choose PDF to JPG when small files matter more."

    answer: "Normal draws pages at 150 dpi, so an A4 page becomes 1240 by 1755 pixels. High uses 300 dpi, twice the detail in each direction, for printing."

Converting PDF to PNG turns pages into lossless pictures, so text, charts and diagrams stay crisp in slides, documents and websites. Save every page as a PNG, with white paper or a see-through background, or extract just the pictures placed inside the PDF. Choose Normal for screens or High for print, and download one picture or all of them in one ZIP.
