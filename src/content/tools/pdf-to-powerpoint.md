Converting a PDF to PowerPoint turns each page into a slide that looks just like it. The page's pictures, shapes and colours sit behind, and its text goes on top in boxes you can edit, in the same fonts, sizes and colours. Convert one PDF or many at once, and nothing ever leaves your device.
