Converting a PDF to text takes every word out of it, in reading order, as a plain .txt file you can copy, edit, search or paste anywhere. Lines and paragraphs are kept, and Bangla comes out as real Bangla letters. Convert one PDF or many at once, and nothing ever leaves your device.
