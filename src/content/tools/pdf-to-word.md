faq:
  - question: "Does this upload my PDF?"
    answer: "No. The PDF is read and the Word document is built inside your browser, then saved straight to your device. You can check this in your browser's Network tab: nothing is uploaded."
  - question: "Will it look exactly like the PDF?"
    answer: "Very close. Each page keeps its size, and text keeps its font, size, bold and italic, with pictures in place. Lines join back into paragraphs that flow, so editing is easy. Drawn lines and shapes, like table borders, aren't brought across."
  - question: "Why are my pages pictures instead of text?"
    answer: "The PDF is a scan or a photo, so there's no text in it to edit. Run it through OCR PDF first, then convert the copy it gives you."
  - question: "Can it convert Bangla PDFs?"
    answer: "Yes, when the PDF holds real Bangla text. PDFs typed with old Bijoy fonts store English letters in place of Bangla, and those come out the same way."
  - question: "Which programs open the file?"
    answer: "Microsoft Word, Google Docs, LibreOffice, WPS Office and Apple Pages all open .docx files, on computers and phones."
  - question: "Does it work offline?"
    answer: "Yes, once your files are chosen. The page loads its PDF tools as soon as you start choosing files. After that, you can turn off your connection and still convert them."
---

Converting a PDF to Word turns it into a document you can edit. Lines join back into paragraphs that flow, and fonts, sizes, bold, italic, pictures and each page's size stay as they were. Bangla comes out as real Bangla letters. Convert one PDF or many at once, and nothing ever leaves your device.
