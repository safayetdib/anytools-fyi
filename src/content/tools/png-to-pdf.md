---

Converting PNG to PDF turns screenshots, diagrams and logos into a document that's easy to share, upload or print. PNG images go into the PDF pixel for pixel, so text in screenshots stays sharp and transparent backgrounds are kept. Use Fit to keep each image's own size, or A4 and US Letter for pages that print evenly.
