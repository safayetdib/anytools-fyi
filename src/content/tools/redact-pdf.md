faq:
  - question: "Does this upload my PDF?"
    answer: "No. Your PDF is redacted inside your browser and saved straight to your device. You can check this in your browser's Network tab: nothing is uploaded."
  - question: "Is the hidden text really gone?"
    answer: "Yes. Each marked page is redrawn as a picture with the boxes painted in, and the new file is built without the old page. No text, picture or link from under a box is left in it."
  - question: "Why can't I select text on a redacted page?"
    answer: "Marked pages become pictures, which is what makes the redaction safe. Pages with nothing marked keep their text. To search a redacted page again, run it through OCR PDF."
  - question: "Can it find words for me?"
    answer: "Yes. Type a name, number or phrase and select Mark all to box every place it's typed in the PDF. Scanned pages have no text to search, so drag over those words instead."
  - question: "Are the document's details removed too?"
    answer: "Yes. The title, author and other details stored with the PDF aren't copied into the redacted file, since they often hold names."
  - question: "Can I undo a redaction later?"
    answer: "No, and that's the point: once saved, what was under the boxes can't be recovered from the new file. Your original file isn't changed."
---

Redacting a PDF removes private details for good, such as account numbers, names, addresses or signatures, before you share it. Find words by typing them, or drag over any area, and each marked page is redrawn with solid black boxes, so what was under them isn't in the file at all. Nothing ever leaves your device.
