Removing a PDF's metadata takes out the details it keeps about itself: its title, author, subject and keywords, the apps that made it, its dates, and the camera and place details inside its photos. The pages stay exactly as they are. Clean one PDF or many at once before you share them, and nothing ever leaves your device.
