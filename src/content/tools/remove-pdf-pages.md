---

Removing pages makes a new PDF without the pages you don't need, like a blank page from a scan, an old cover page, or pages a form doesn't ask for. Tap the pages to remove, or type their numbers, and download the rest. Every page you keep stays at its original quality, in the same order, and your original file isn't changed.
