Repairing a PDF fixes files that won't open, show errors or come out blank, most often because a download was cut short or another app saved the file badly. The file's structure is rebuilt from the pages still inside it, so you can read, print and share it again. Repair one PDF or many at once, and nothing ever leaves your device.
