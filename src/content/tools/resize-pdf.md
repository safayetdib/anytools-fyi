faq:
  - question: "Does this upload my PDF?"
    answer: "No. The pages are resized inside your browser and the PDF is saved straight to your device. You can check this in your browser's Network tab: nothing is uploaded."
  - question: "Will my pages be stretched or cut off?"
    answer: "No. Each page is scaled evenly to fit inside the paper and its margin, then centred, so it keeps its shape and nothing is lost."
  - question: "Does the text stay sharp?"
    answer: "Yes. Text and drawings stay as text and drawings, so they're as sharp as before at any size."
  - question: "Do links and form fields still work?"
    answer: "With one page to a sheet, yes: links, form fields and comments move with their page. With several pages to a sheet, pages are drawn small side by side, so flatten any forms first."
  - question: "Can I put several pages on one sheet?"
    answer: "Yes. Choose 2, 4, 6 or 9 pages per sheet. They're laid out in reading order, left to right and top to bottom."
  - question: "Does it work offline?"
    answer: "Yes, once your files are chosen. The page loads its PDF tools as soon as you start choosing files. After that, you can turn off your connection and still resize them."

Resizing a PDF fits every page onto one paper size, like A4 or Letter, scaled to keep its shape and centred, so nothing is cut off or stretched. Add a margin, or put 2 to 9 pages on each sheet for handouts. Resize one PDF or many at once, and nothing ever leaves your device.
