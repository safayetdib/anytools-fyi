Rotating turns the pages of a PDF so they read the right way up, like a scan that came out sideways or a photo of a form taken upside down. Turn one file or many at once, right or left, or only the portrait or landscape ones. The turn is saved into the new PDF, so it opens correctly everywhere, and every page keeps its original quality.
