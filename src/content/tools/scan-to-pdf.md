faq:
  - question: "Does this upload my photos?"
    answer: "No. Your photos are cleaned and turned into a PDF inside your browser, and saved straight to your device. You can check this in your browser's Network tab: nothing is uploaded."
  - question: "Do I need to install an app?"
    answer: "No. It works in your phone's browser. Select Take a photo to open the camera, or choose photos already on your phone."
  - question: "What do Colour and Black and white do?"
    answer: "Both even out the light across the page, remove shadows and turn grey or yellowed paper white. Colour keeps stamps, signatures and photos in colour; Black and white turns everything into crisp black text on white."
  - question: "Can I scan several pages into one PDF?"
    answer: "Yes. Take a photo of each page, put them in order, and they all go into one PDF. Untick Merge to get one PDF per page instead."
  - question: "Can I scan Bangla documents?"
    answer: "Yes. The page is kept as a picture, so any language and handwriting look exactly as on paper."
  - question: "Will the text be searchable?"
    answer: "No. Each page is a picture of the paper, like with most scanners. The text can be read and printed, but not selected or searched."
---

Scanning to PDF turns photos of paper into a clean document you can email, print or upload to an online form. Take a photo of each page with your phone, and the shadows, uneven light and grey paper are cleaned away, in colour or black and white. Put the pages in order, choose A4 or Letter, and get one PDF. No app to install, and your photos never leave your phone.
