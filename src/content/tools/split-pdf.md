Splitting turns one PDF into several smaller ones. It's useful when a form or portal asks for one document from a bundle, like a single certificate or transcript page, when a file is too large to email or upload, or when a long scan holds several documents. Split by page ranges, extract every page or just the ones you pick, or keep every file under a size limit. Every page stays at its original quality, and your original file isn't changed.
