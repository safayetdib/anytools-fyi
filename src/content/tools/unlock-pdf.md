Unlocking a PDF removes its password, so it opens without asking every time, and lifts limits on printing, copying and editing. It's handy for bank statements, payslips and bills that arrive locked. Type the password once, unlock one PDF or many, and download them. Everything happens in your browser, so neither your files nor your passwords leave your device.
