faq:
  - question: "Does this upload my WebP files?"
    answer: "No. Your images are read and turned into a PDF inside your browser, and the PDF is saved straight to your device. Nothing is uploaded."

---

Converting WebP to PDF turns pictures saved from websites into a document that opens on any phone, computer or printer. Many apps still can't open WebP files, but everything opens a PDF. Choose A4 or US Letter to print, or Fit to keep each picture's own shape, and merge several images into one file.
