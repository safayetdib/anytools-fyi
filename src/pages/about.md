AnyTools.fyi is a set of free everyday tools for files, images, text and Bangla. Every tool runs inside your browser, so your files stay on your device.

## Why we built it

Many online tools upload your files to their servers, add watermarks or ask you to sign up. We think a quick job, like merging two PDFs or shrinking a photo for a form, shouldn't cost you your privacy.

## How it works

When you open a tool, your browser downloads the page and the tool's code. After that, the work happens on your phone or computer. [See how we protect your privacy](/how-we-protect-your-privacy/).

## Who it's for

Anyone who needs a job done quickly, in English or Bangla, on a phone or a laptop. The site is built to work with a keyboard and with screen readers too.
