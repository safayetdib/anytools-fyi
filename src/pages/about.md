AnyTools is a set of free tools for everyday PDF jobs, with no signup and no watermarks. We add new tools one at a time, once each one is ready.

## Why we built it

Many online tools upload your files to their servers, add watermarks or ask you to sign up. We think a quick job, like merging two PDFs or shrinking a photo for a form, shouldn't cost you your privacy.

## How it works

Where a job can be done on your phone or computer, the tool does it there, and its page says so near the top. [See how we protect your privacy](/how-we-protect-your-privacy/).

## Who it's for

Anyone who needs a job done quickly, on a phone or a laptop. The site is built to work with a keyboard and with screen readers too.
