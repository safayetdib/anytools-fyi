We want every tool to work for everyone, including people who use a keyboard, a screen reader or a zoomed screen.

## Our goal

Every page is built to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA. We test with automated checks on every change, and by hand with a keyboard, at 200% zoom and in dark mode.

## What you can expect

- Every tool can be used with a keyboard alone.
- Files can be chosen with a normal file picker. Drag and drop is never required.
- Progress and results are announced to screen readers.
- The site follows your device's light or dark setting and its reduced motion setting.

## Report a problem

If something doesn't work for you, please tell us through our [contact page](/contact/). We fix accessibility problems first.
