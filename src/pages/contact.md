We'd like to hear about bugs, accessibility problems and ideas for new tools.

## Email

Write to [safayetdib@gmail.com](mailto:safayetdib@gmail.com). This is also the address for privacy questions and requests.

When you report a bug, it helps to tell us the tool, your device and your browser.

## Who's behind AnyTools

AnyTools is made by Safayet Adib. See more of Safayet's work at [safayetadib.com](https://safayetadib.com/).

Please don't send us files you used with a tool. We never need them, and we don't keep them.
