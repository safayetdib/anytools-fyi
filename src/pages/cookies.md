The tools themselves don't need cookies. We only set cookies if you choose **Accept** in the cookie banner, and only for Google Analytics, which tells us how many people visit and which tools they use.

## Cookies, only if you accept

| Name | Set by | What it's for | Kept for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `_ga` | Google Analytics | Tells one visit from another, without knowing who you are | 2 years |
| `_ga_5KMBSHH44P` | Google Analytics | Keeps track of the current visit | 2 years |

If you choose **Decline**, Google Analytics isn't loaded at all, and nothing is sent to Google.

## Kept in your browser, not cookies

These never leave your device:

| Name | What it's for |
| --- | --- |
| `anytools-consent` | Remembers whether you accepted or declined cookies |
| `theme` | Remembers light or dark mode, if you chose one |
| `logoIntro` | Plays the logo animation once per visit. It's cleared when you close the tab |

## Change your choice

Select **Cookie settings** at the bottom of any page to accept or decline again. Declining removes the Google Analytics cookies. You can also clear this site's data in your browser's settings at any time.
