## Your files

A tool that works entirely in your browser says so at the top of its page. With those tools, your files and the text you type are processed on your device. They are not uploaded to our servers, and we never see them.

## Visits

If you choose **Accept** in the cookie banner, we use Google Analytics to understand how the site is used. It collects:

- the pages you visit and the site that sent you
- your device type, browser and approximate location, such as country or city
- that a tool finished its job, with the tool's page address

It never receives your files, file names, page counts or anything you type. We've turned off Google signals and ad personalisation, and we don't use the data for advertising.

If you choose **Decline**, or make no choice, Google Analytics isn't loaded and nothing is sent to Google. [Cookies](/cookies/) lists every cookie and what it's for.

## Hosting

The site is served by Cloudflare. Like any web host, Cloudflare handles your IP address to deliver pages and protect the site from attacks.

## Your choices

- Select **Cookie settings** at the bottom of any page to change your answer at any time.
- Browser privacy settings and content blockers also stop Google Analytics.

## Contact

Questions about privacy can be sent through our [contact page](/contact/).
